## 3. What you share is your choice

Nothing you record leaves your phone unless you switch it on. You choose, separately:

- which categories are backed up to the cloud (cars, events, personal information, GPS

- who can see what you share — nobody, your friends, or everyone;
- which individual cars and events are shared at all.

Two things are **never** shareable, by design and not merely by default: your full GPS
history and your overall driving statistics. They can be backed up, encrypted, but there is
no setting that shows them to another person. Only the GPS trace attached to an event you
have explicitly shared is ever visible to others.

Bear in mind that anything you do share may be seen, remembered, or screenshotted by the
people you shared it with. Sharing a trace shows where you drove. Think about whether that
includes your home.
